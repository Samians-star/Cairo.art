Course

Graphic Design

Core design thinking — typography, layout and branding — applied through a real personal design project.

Apply for This Course ← All Courses

Who it's for

Beginners and self-taught designers wanting formal design fundamentals.

Requirements

No prior software experience required; guidance on tools provided in class.

Materials

Laptop (design software guidance provided in class).

What you'll learn

  • Strong grasp of layout, contrast and hierarchy
  • Typography fundamentals
  • A basic personal branding project

A Certificate of Completion is issued once you finish the programme.

Curriculum

8-Week Programme Outline

A proposed starting structure — your teacher may adapt pacing to the class.

Week 1
Design principles: balance, contrast, hierarchy
Week 2
Typography fundamentals
Week 3
Colour theory for design
Week 4
Layout and grid systems
Week 5
Branding and logo design basics
Week 6
Digital tools workflow
Week 7
Personal branding project
Week 8
Portfolio piece and presentation
