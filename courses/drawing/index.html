Course

Drawing

A complete observational drawing foundation — line, form, light and composition — building toward confident original work.

Apply for This Course ← All Courses

Who it's for

Beginners building a foundation, and intermediate students sharpening technique.

Requirements

No prior experience needed.

Materials

Graphite pencils (HB–6B), Eraser, Sketchbook, Blending tools.

What you'll learn

  • Accurate observational line work
  • Understanding of proportion and perspective
  • Confident shading and light control
  • A finished, original composition

A Certificate of Completion is issued once you finish the programme.

Curriculum

8-Week Programme Outline

A proposed starting structure — your teacher may adapt pacing to the class.

Week 1
Observational drawing and line fundamentals
Week 2
Shape and form studies
Week 3
Proportion and measurement technique
Week 4
Perspective (one- and two-point)
Week 5
Light, shadow and shading technique
Week 6
Still life composition
Week 7
Portrait and figure drawing basics
Week 8
Final composition and critique
