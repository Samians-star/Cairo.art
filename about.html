Our story

Founded on the belief that craft still matters

Cairo Art Studio was founded in 2018 as a home for serious, hands-on art education — calligraphy, drawing, painting, photography, design and more. Cairo School of Art & Calligraphy carries that same practice forward, in collaboration with Al Azhar Fine Art Studio, across Lahore and Islamabad.

Every programme is built the same way: real technique, real feedback, and a finished piece to show for it.

What we teach

Fifteen disciplines, one standard

Calligraphy, Handwriting (English, Urdu, Arabic), Drawing, Painting, Photography, Graphic Design, Miniature Painting, Sculpture, Texture Painting, Multimedia/Film & TV, Fashion Design and Spoken English.
